Ghanaian Damii Rules

• 10×10 board, only dark squares used

• 20 pieces each side

• Men move diagonally forward 1 square

• Men can capture forward and backward

• Capture is compulsory

• When multiple captures exist, you choose freely (not forced to take longest)

• Reach opposite side → becomes Flying King (any distance)

• Win by capturing all or blocking opponent

Prizes and leaderboard credit for beating the AI only count if you didn't use Undo.
