🎱 Kaisa Challenge

Settings for the Kaisa pool game (kaisa.html). Balls, prices and social links above are shared with Foosball. Beating the AI on Easy is practice only — the prize and leaderboard credit apply to Normal and Hard.

♟️ Damii (Ghanaian Draughts)

Settings for damii.html. Playing vs AI is a 3-level campaign: beat the Hard AI, choose to continue, beat it again, continue again, then beat it a third time in a row (without using Undo in any of the three) to earn the prize and a leaderboard win. Losing at any level deducts 2 balls from the player and ends the run; winning costs nothing.

🎲 Ludo

Settings for ludo.html. Playing vs AI is a 3-level campaign: Level 1 is 1 Hard AI, Level 2 is 2 Hard AIs, Level 3 is 3 Hard AIs — beat a level, choose to continue, and only completing Level 3 earns the prize and a leaderboard win. Losing at any level deducts 2 balls from the player and ends the run; winning costs nothing.

🌐 Connection (TURN relay)

Kaisa and Damii already fall back to a built-in relay automatically, so online matches always work. To also make the fast direct link work on strict mobile networks (and to help Foosball, which has no built-in relay), add a TURN server: create a free account at metered.ca → your app → copy the ICE servers JSON and paste it here. Leave empty to use STUN only.

🔊 Sound Effects

Upload short audio clips for real match-style sound. Each one is used automatically at the matching moment during play — no manual triggering needed.

🧊 3D Player Models (Experimental)

Only used by the separate index-3d.html test version of the game — your regular live game is unaffected either way. Upload the rigged, animated .glb files exported from Tripo (must include a "kick" animation).

📢 Site Announcements

Shown at the very top of every page — Foosball, Kaisa, Damii, Ludo and Chat — the next time each player opens it. Add a message, an image from your gallery, a voice recording, or any mix of the three (at least one is required).
